Data Visualization Guide

Create beautiful and insightful visualizations of your data - no coding needed!

Understanding Visualizations

Data visualizations help you see patterns and trends in your data. Think of them as pictures that tell the story of your business.

Creating Visualizations

1

Select Your Data

Choose what to visualize:

  • Open your dataset
  • Select columns to visualize
  • Choose time period (if applicable)

You can visualize multiple columns at once to see relationships.

2

Choose Chart Type

Pick the best way to show your data:

  • Click "Create Chart"
  • Browse chart options
  • Preview different styles

Our system will suggest the best chart types for your data.

3

Customize Your Chart

Make it your own:

  • Change colors and styles
  • Add titles and labels
  • Adjust size and layout
  • Set interactive features

Use your brand colors to make charts match your style.

4

Share and Export

Use your visualizations:

  • Save to dashboard
  • Download as image
  • Share with team
  • Add to reports

Charts can be exported in various formats for presentations.

Available Chart Types

Bar Charts

Perfect for comparing values:

  • Sales by category
  • Performance comparison
  • Survey responses
  • Rankings

Line Charts

Best for showing trends:

  • Sales over time
  • Growth trends
  • Temperature changes
  • Progress tracking

Pie Charts

Show parts of a whole:

  • Market share
  • Budget allocation
  • Customer segments
  • Time distribution

Scatter Plots

Find relationships:

  • Price vs. sales
  • Age vs. spending
  • Time vs. performance
  • Correlation analysis

Common Questions

Which chart should I use?

Our system will suggest the best chart types based on your data. Generally, use bar charts for comparisons, line charts for trends, and pie charts for parts of a whole.

Can I combine multiple charts?

Yes! Create dashboards with multiple charts to show different aspects of your data side by side.

How do I share my visualizations?

You can share charts and dashboards via email, export them as images, or give team members direct access to view them on the platform.

Can charts update automatically?

Yes! Set up auto-refresh intervals or schedule updates to keep your visualizations current with the latest data.
