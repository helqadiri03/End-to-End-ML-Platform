Data Upload Guide

Learn how to easily upload and manage your data files on the NextData Platform.

Before You Start

The platform accepts the following file types:

  • Excel files (.xlsx, .xls)
  • CSV files (.csv)
  • Text files (.txt)

Uploading Your Data

1

Access Upload Page

Click the "Upload Data" button on your dashboard or use the "+" button in the navigation menu.

You can find the upload button in the top-right corner of your screen.

2

Choose Your File

You can upload your file in two ways:

  • Drag and drop your file into the upload area
  • Click "Browse" to select a file from your computer

Maximum file size: 100MB. For larger files, please contact support.

3

Preview Your Data

Before finalizing the upload:

  • Check if your data appears correctly
  • Verify column names and data types
  • Make sure all information is visible

Use the preview window to scroll through your data and ensure everything looks right.

4

Confirm Upload

Add some details about your dataset:

  • Give your dataset a meaningful name
  • Add a brief description (optional)
  • Select data category (optional)
  • Click "Upload" to finish

Editing Details

Update your dataset information:

Sharing Options

Share your data with team members:

  • Share with specific users

Best Practices

Review your file before uploading to ensure it's complete and correct

Use folders and tags to keep your data well-organized

Common Questions

What happens if my upload fails?

Don't worry! The platform will show you what went wrong and how to fix it. You can try uploading again after making the necessary corrections.

Can I upload multiple files at once?

Yes! You can select multiple files or drag several files at once into the upload area.

What if my file is too large?

Contact our support team for assistance with files larger than 100MB. We can help you find the best solution for your needs.

Can I update an existing dataset?

Yes! Select the dataset you want to update and use the "Update" option to upload a new version while keeping the history.

Need help uploading your data? Try our Interactive Upload Guide or contact our support team for assistance.
