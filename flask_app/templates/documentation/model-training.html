Training Your Model

Learn how to train AI models on your data - no coding required!

Understanding Model Training

Model training is where your data teaches our AI system to make predictions. Think of it like teaching a new employee by showing them examples of past work.

Getting Started

1

Select Your Data

Choose the dataset you want to use for training:

  • Click "Train Model" from your dashboard
  • Select your cleaned dataset
  • Verify that your data is ready

Make sure your data is cleaned before training. Use our data cleaning tool if needed.

2

Choose Your Goal

Tell us what you want to predict:

  • Select your target (what you want to predict)
  • Choose prediction type (e.g., sales forecast, customer behavior)
  • Pick relevant features (what information to use)

Our system will automatically suggest the best options based on your data.

3

Configure Training

Customize your training settings:

  • Choose training speed (faster vs. more accurate)
  • Set validation preferences
  • Enable advanced options (optional)

Default settings work well for most cases - you don't need to change them!

4

Start Training

Launch the training process:

  • Review your settings
  • Click "Start Training"
  • Monitor progress

Training time varies based on data size. You'll get a notification when it's done!

Training Options

Standard Train

Balanced approach:

  • Better accuracy
  • More features

  • Most common choice

Advanced Train

For complex needs:

  • Highest accuracy

Common Questions

How long does training take?

Training time varies from minutes to hours depending on data size and settings. We'll notify you when it's done!

What if training fails?

Don't worry! We'll show you what went wrong and how to fix it. Most issues are easily resolved by adjusting settings or cleaning data.

Which training option should I choose?

Start with Quick Train for your first model. If you need better accuracy, try Standard or Advanced options.

Can I stop training midway?

Yes! You can pause or stop training anytime. Your progress will be saved.

Need help with model training? Try our Interactive Training Guide or contact our support team.
