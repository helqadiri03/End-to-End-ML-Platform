Understanding AI Models

AI models are tools that learn from your data to make predictions. Think of them as smart assistants that can help automate your business decisions.

Choosing the Right Model

1

Identify Your Goal

What do you want to predict?

  • Future values (like sales or demand)
  • Categories (like customer segments)
  • Yes/No decisions (like approval/rejection)
  • Patterns in data (like customer behavior)

Our system will suggest the best model based on your goal.

2

Check Your Data

What kind of data do you have?

  • Numbers (sales, prices, quantities)
  • Text (customer feedback, descriptions)
  • Dates (transaction history, timelines)
  • Categories (product types, locations)

Different models work better with different types of data.

3

Select Model Type

Browse our model categories:

  • Click "Create New Model"
  • Answer simple questions about your needs
  • Review recommended models
  • Choose the best fit

Our AI assistant will guide you to the perfect model choice.
