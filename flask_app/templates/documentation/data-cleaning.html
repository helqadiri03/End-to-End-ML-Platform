Common Questions

How long does cleaning take?

Most automatic cleaning tasks take just a few minutes. The exact time depends on your data size and the types of cleaning needed.

Can I undo cleaning steps?

Yes! Every change is recorded in the history. You can review and undo any changes you've made.

What if I'm not sure about a change?

Use the "Preview" feature to see how changes will affect your data before applying them. You can also save versions of your data at any point.

Will cleaning delete my data?

No, we always keep your original data safe. You can always go back to it if needed.
