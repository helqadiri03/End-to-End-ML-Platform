Evaluating Your Model

Learn how to check your model's performance and understand its predictions.

Understanding Model Evaluation

Model evaluation helps you understand how well your AI model is performing. Think of it like a report card that shows how accurately your model can make predictions.

Viewing Results

1

Access Results Dashboard

Find your model's performance:

  • Go to "My Models" section
  • Select your trained model
  • Click "View Results"

Results are automatically generated after training completes.

2

Understand Performance

Review key metrics:

  • Overall accuracy score
  • Prediction confidence
  • Error analysis
  • Visual performance charts

Higher scores mean better performance. We highlight areas that need attention.

3

Test Predictions

Try your model on new data:

  • Use "Test Model" feature
  • Upload test data
  • View predictions
  • Compare with actual results

Testing helps ensure your model works well on new, unseen data.

4

Export Results

Save and share your findings:

  • Download performance reports
  • Export visualizations
  • Share with team members
  • Save model versions

Reports are available in PDF and Excel formats for easy sharing.

Common Questions

What's a good accuracy score?

It depends on your specific case, but generally, scores above 80% are considered good. We'll help you understand what's realistic for your data.

Why did my model's performance drop?

Performance can change if your data changes or if new patterns emerge. We'll help you identify and fix the cause.

How often should I evaluate my model?

Regular evaluation is important - we recommend checking performance at least monthly or whenever your data significantly changes.

Can I compare different models?

Yes! Our comparison tool lets you see different models side by side to choose the best one for your needs.
