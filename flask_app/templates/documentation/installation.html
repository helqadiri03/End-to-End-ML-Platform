Getting Started with NextData Platform

Start using our platform in minutes with this simple setup guide.

System Requirements

Computer & Browser

  • Any modern computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux)
  • Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge browser
  • Stable internet connection

Display

  • Minimum resolution: 1280 x 720
  • Recommended: 1920 x 1080 or higher
  • Works on tablets too!

Getting Started

1

Create Your Account

Sign up in minutes:

  • Click "Sign Up" at the top right
  • Enter your business email
  • Choose a secure password
  • Verify your email

Your data is always secure with our enterprise-grade encryption.

2

Complete Your Profile

Tell us about your business:

  • Add your company details
  • Choose your industry
  • Set your preferences
  • Invite team members

Team members can be added anytime from your account settings.

3

Upload Your First Dataset

Start with your data:

  • Click "Upload Data"
  • Choose your file
  • Review the preview
  • Confirm upload

We support Excel, CSV, and many other common formats.
